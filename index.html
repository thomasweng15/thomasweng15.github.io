Hi, I am a robotics research scientist at the Robotics and AI Institute (formerly the Boston Dynamics AI Institute). My research focuses on developing sample-efficient algorithms that enable robots to learn and adapt in the real world, with the goal of enabling robots to perform diverse manipulation tasks in unstructured human environments.

Prior to my current role, I completed my PhD with with Prof. Dave Held at the CMU Robotics Institute. I also previously worked on human-robot interaction with Prof. Maya Cakmak at UW and Prof. Brian Scassellati at Yale.
